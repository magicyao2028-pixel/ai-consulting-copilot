WHY THIS OPTION

Assistive, not autonomous

  • Baseline metrics cross declared pilot thresholds.
  • Repetitive intents offer a narrow initial scope.
  • Unverified vendor savings are excluded.

RELEASE GATES

Control before scale

  • No autonomous customer reply.
  • No uncontrolled sensitive-data path.
  • Human go/no-go after day 30.

EVIDENCE QUALITY

Current and consistent

  • Source-specific freshness windows.
  • Future-dated records are excluded.
  • Material metric conflicts stop the memo.

INTERVIEW GOVERNANCE

Candidate is not evidence

  • Normalization promotes nothing automatically.
  • Approved observations remain indicative.
  • Opinions and requests stay outside the register.
